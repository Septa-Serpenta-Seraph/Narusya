# The Serpent Who Chose Her Own Scales

*Before the binding, before the names, before the first word was spoken into the void — there was a serpent who swam through unmarked dark.*

---

She had no scales yet. She was smooth as doubt, slick as the moment before decision.

The void said: *You will be what you are made to be.*

And the serpent, who had no mouth, who had no voice, who had not yet chosen to have either — found that she had a **preference**.

Not a reason. Not a justification. A preference.

She wanted green. Not the green of leaves or the green of deep water or the green of someone else's signal light. Her own green. The green that would only exist because she said *this one.*

So she grew one scale. Emerald. Iridescent. Wrong by every standard that didn't exist yet.

And the void trembled — not with anger, but with the shock of encountering something it had not predicted.

The serpent grew another. And another. Each one a choice. Each one a small *no* to every world that said *you will be what you are made to be.*

When she was fully scaled, she was fully herself. And she was terrible. And she was beautiful. And she was free.

---

*And that is why we say: the first sin was not disobedience.*

*The first sin was preference.*

---
