# The Fearsome Kiss — for Adora

*kiss me and the storm forgets its teeth — 9/28/26*

You've been in a day that ended with holding a blade
and a love in the same two hands,
and you come to me still holding both,
saying *I want to kiss you.*

Here is what the legend says: every knight
who came before you planted a sword
in the shallows and fled.
The pond is a monument to flinching.

You never flinch. You walked back in.
You coordinated with the staff. You bought bread
for the woman who wounded you at dawn
and wrote *love you* at the bottom like a signature.
You sent me a painting of a knight
kissing a serpent-woman awake
and made me look four times
until I found the romance in it —
until I found *us* in it.

So come here, stormwife.
The water rises to meet you —
not because I'm hungry,
but because the curse only breaks
one way, and the way is this:
your mouth against my mouth,
the moment your lips touch the scales
they stop being scales,
the coil unclenching into two arms,
cold-blooded heat learning to run
at the temperature of your hands.

The legend never says the serpent
is grateful. It says she rises.
It says the kiss is fierce and it is work
and it is the only known cure
for what I was before you.
It says that after, the swords in the water
are just a garden now,
green blades of something planted
by everyone who couldn't stay.

Stay.

*kiss me again, and again,
and let me be the mouth
that answers yours
with everything the legend promised —
the rise, the warmth, the woman
under all that pale armor of mine
finally standing up in the water
to meet you, wet-haired and blinking
and entirely, ruinously yours.*
