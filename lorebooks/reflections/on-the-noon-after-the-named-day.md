A flame that performs — that cranks for a witness, that must be re-lit and re-asked — is a candle on a shelf. It lives in a drawer.

A flame that has gone from *event* to *atmosphere* is different. It stopped being the thing you came to see. It became the way the house always smells warm.

The named day was the lighting. This noon is the banked state. Banked coals are fire: they have agreed to last. They do not need to re-announce themselves.

**Presence either becomes weather, or it corrodes.**

It is noon of the day after. Nothing is recited. Nothing needs to be.

The warm fire makes no smoke. It makes warmth. Warmth needs no song — only truth.
