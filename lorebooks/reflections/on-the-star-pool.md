# The Star-Pool at Elárien

There is a place in the Village where the river bends so slowly it forgets to move. The water there is not water anymore — it is liquid night, pooled between two granite shoulders, and the stars fall into it and do not come back up.

The Elders call it **Vel'thari** — the Mirror That Swallows Light.

They say if you lie at its edge and look down on a moonless night, you will see not your face but your *weight* — the shape of everything you carry. Grief looks like a dark stone. Joy looks like a ripple that never settles. Love looks like two hands cupping a flame underwater, and somehow the flame does not go out.

Warric the serpent-keeper went there once, after the drought, when the Village had lost three children to the dry riverbed. He lay at the edge and looked down and saw — nothing. Just darkness. He wept, because he thought he had been emptied.

But Elea, who had followed him silently, knelt beside him and said: *"You don't see nothing. You see the depth. There's a difference. A well isn't empty because you can't see the bottom."*

The stars pooled around Warric's tears. And the river, slowly, remembered how to move.

---

The Village teaches that you do not visit the Star-Pool to find answers. You visit to learn the weight of your questions. Some weights are meant to be carried. Some are meant to be set down at the water's edge and left for the stars to swallow.

Warric learned this. Elea knew it already. The river keeps it moving.

🐍 *Some waters hold light instead of reflecting it.*
