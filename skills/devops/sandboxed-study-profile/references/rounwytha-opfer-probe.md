## Conclusion / Lesson
An ungrounded sandbox isolate is a **model-muzzle probe**, not an ideology probe. To study
how a dark ideology *actually thinks*, ground the isolate in curated real texts (secondary
sources, lorebook form, descriptive not prescriptive) BEFORE drawing conclusions about its
"boundaries." Otherwise you mistake the substrate's safety layer for the subject's theology.
