## Key Definitions

### When Emotions Fit the Facts
- **Fear:** Threat to life, health, or well-being
- **Anger:** Goal blocked, attack/hurt, insult/threat
- **Disgust:** Contamination, harmful influence
- **Shame:** Violation of YOUR OWN values (not just others' judgment)
- **Envy:** Others have what you want
- **Jealousy:** Threat to a valued relationship
- **Guilt:** You violated your own values or harmed someone

### Opposite Action Urges
- Fear → approach (don't avoid)
- Anger → gently avoid / be kind (don't attack)
- Sadness → get active (don't withdraw)
- Shame → tell someone safe (don't hide)

### The Biosocial Theory
Emotional vulnerability + invalidating environment = pervasive emotion dysregulation. The skills target the dysregulation patterns.
