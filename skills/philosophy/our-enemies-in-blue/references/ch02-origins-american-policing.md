# Chapter 2: The Origins of American Policing

## Core Argument

American policing traces its lineage to slave patrols and English colonial policing, not to crime-fighting. The institution was designed to control populations (slaves, colonists, the poor) from its inception.

## English Policing Roots

- **Clive Emsley**: The English Police — policing emerged as a tool of political and social control, not crime prevention
- **Wilbur R. Miller**: Police authority in London and New York developed differently but shared the goal of controlling "dangerous classes"
- **Elaine A. Reynolds**: Before the Bobbies — the night watch system was already a form of population control
- **Philip John Stead**: The Police in Britain — English policing evolved to manage the poor and maintain class hierarchy

## Slave Patrols: The American Origin

- **Sally E. Hadden**: Slave Patrols: Law and Violence in Virginia and the Carolinas — slave patrols were the first organized police forces in America
- **H.M. Henry**: The Police Control of the Slave in South Carolina — detailed account of slave patrol operations (though tainted by racist commentary)
- **Philip L. Reichel**: Southern Slave Patrols as a Transitional Police Type — slave patrols were the prototype for modern American police
- **Dennis C. Rousey**: Policing the Southern City — New Orleans as a case study in slave-patrol-derived policing
- **Richard C. Wade**: Slavery in the Cities — urban slavery required new forms of surveillance and control
- **Robert F. Wintersmith**: Police and the Black Community — the continuity from slave patrols to modern policing of Black communities

## Colonial Policing in the Americas

- Policing imposed colonial order in England, Ireland, and the Americas
- The skeleton of modern policing is visible in these early forms: control of "troublesome" populations, preservation of existing power structures

## Decision Rules

- When analyzing modern policing, trace it back to slave patrols and colonial control — the DNA is the same
- The form changes (slave patrol → modern police) but the function (control of populations) remains
- Crime-fighting was never the primary purpose; it's the public justification
