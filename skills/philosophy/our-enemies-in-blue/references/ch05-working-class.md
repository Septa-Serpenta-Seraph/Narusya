# Chapter 5: The Natural Enemy of the Working Class

## Core Argument

Police exist to stifle the social ambitions of workers and maintain the power of capital. They break strikes, bust unions, and use violence to prevent the working class from organizing. This is not a perversion of police function — it IS the function.

## Police and Strikebreaking

- **George Orwell**: Homage to Catalonia — the police serve the interests of the ruling class
- **Sidney Harring**: Policing a Class Society — police handled labor disputes in favor of capital; strikebreaking was a primary function
- **Jeremy Brecher**: Strike! — the Klan burned crosses near factories to intimidate striking workers; police and Klan collaborated against labor
- **State police as strikebreakers**: Pennsylvania State Police, Texas Rangers, and other forces were explicitly created in part to handle labor unrest

## The Reproduction of the Working Class

- **Harring**: "Capital's turn to the police to handle some aspects of the reproduction of the working class cannot be separated from a more general move by the bourgeoisie...to use public institutions in general for that purpose"
- Police, education, public health, welfare — all are tools for controlling the working class and ensuring the reproduction of capital
- Post-Civil War period saw rapid expansion of these efforts

## Anti-Union Violence

- **Pinkertons**: Private police forces used as strikebreakers; agents infiltrated unions and aided in the commission of crimes to discredit them (Molly Maguires case, 1877)
- **State forces**: deputized scabs, banned public assemblies, mass arrests, physical attacks on strikers
- **1919 Steel Strike**: 20 killed, strike collapsed with workers winning nothing
- **Haymarket affair**: similar pattern of anti-union violence followed by show trials

## Class Bias in Policing

- **Anatole France**: "The law, in its majestic equality, forbids rich and poor alike to sleep under bridges, to beg in the streets, and to steal bread"
- **George Orwell**: police are the natural enemy of the working class
- **Stansell**: the campaign against prostitution targeted laboring women — the moral panic was about controlling the milieu from which prostitutes came

## Decision Rules

- When analyzing police actions during labor disputes, recognize that police are not neutral — they serve capital
- When evaluating "public order" justifications, ask: whose order? Whose interests?
- When studying the history of labor, include police violence as a key factor in union busting
- When police or private security are used against workers, this is the system working as designed
