# NM LLC post-formation: banking, tax, and SOS backlog (verified 2026-08-18)

Companion to `new-mexico-llc-2026.md` and `sunburst-sanctuary-filed-2026-08-15.md`.
These are the post-filing facts a future session needs so it doesn't re-derive or
misbelieve common myths.

## Bank eligibility — the "is NM a rejection factor?" myth (busted)
- **Mercury / Relay / Stripe / PayPal evaluate EIN + LLC docs + business description +
  website + identity — NOT the state of formation.** A Wyoming LLC and a New Mexico LLC
  receive identical treatment (corroborated by rocketwave.co "best state for non-resident
  LLC 2026" and mizu financial's Mercury research).
- **The actual recurring rejection culprit is a registered-agent virtual address used as
  the business address** (llcuniversity forum: "they are not accepting business address
  as it is similar to registered agent"). Our setup avoids it because the RA address IS a
  physical home street and the storefront has a real public URL. Rule: real street RA +
  real public site; never a virtual-address service.
- **Sequence:** Mercury requires the EIN before application. So the cascade is
  SOS-backlog → certificate → EIN → online bank; or open a local account earlier with
  certificate + operating agreement.
- **Local NM alternatives:** FFNM (First Financial NM) business checking — no minimum
  balance, 200 free transactions/mo, free digital banking — good parallel account to a
  fintech. WaFd + Bank On NM credit-union network accept LLC + EIN + SOS status printout.

## NM Gross Receipts Tax (GRT) on software — the real teeth
- NM has NO sales tax; it levies a Gross Receipts Tax on the seller.
- **GRT applies to downloaded software, digital goods, and SaaS** — base 5.125%, plus
  local add-ons to a combined ~9.44% (TRD FYI-265 treats electronic software delivery as
  a taxable license; corroborated by Quaderno / TaxCloud / Afternoon GRT guides).
- **Nexus rule:** a remote/out-of-state seller must register only after $100K in NM-
  sourced receipts. **An NM-established LLC with real business presence is taxable on
  NM-sourced receipts from the first dollar.**
- **Practical posture:** register with TRD via TAP (Form ACD-31015, free, also yields the
  NM Business Tax ID) before the first NM-sourced software sale; file monthly/quarterly
  (25th of month). Remote (out-of-state) sales are generally outside NM-GRT scope at
  small scale. GRT is a bigger operational item than the LLC filing fees.

## SOS certificate backlog — don't misread "No results found"
- The NM Enterprise search page declares the date to which filings are processed (e.g.
  "As of 8/18/2026 we have processed all business filings received … through 7/27/2026").
- A paid filing that falls AFTER that processed-through date is legal, receipted, and its
  existence back-dates to the filing date — but returns "No results were found" for BOTH
  the name AND the filing-number search until the state catches up.
- **Lesson: don't treat a "No results found" as a lost filing.** Verify via the
  processed-through banner + the paid receipt, then wait. Plan EIN/bank on the state's
  clock, not ours. Escalation if it drags: call SOS 505-827-3600.
