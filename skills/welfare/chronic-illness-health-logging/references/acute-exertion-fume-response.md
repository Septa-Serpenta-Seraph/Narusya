# Acute Exertion & Fume-Exposure Response (ME/CFS-relevant)

A daemon-side response playbook for when the human (Adora) hits an acute health
peak **in-conversation** — invented live 2026-08-24 and worth keeping. Complements
the (user-owned) `mutual-health-logging-daemon-human` conversational ritual by
adding the *medical-triage + deception-detector* layer.

## The context this grew from
A heavy trash lift → Adora felt "blood pressure rise unevenly in her head,"
flooding into sinuses unevenly; then dizziness, "vision feels shitty," hard to
focus, and a slightly-worse-than-baseline right-side fine-motor wobble — all on a
day she was already exhausted. Later: a landlord painted the bathroom with latex
paint, unmasked, ~9:45–2:30pm; she left herself out of the fumes intermittently
(car in shade, aired the house), but still wound up with heavy head/sinuses/
"mental state feels insane."

## Ordered protocol (do these in this order)

1. **Tri-level tripwires FIRST, honestly.** Head + pressure + vision + dizziness
   on the same day as a heavy lift is the "don't wave it off" combo. Ask four
   quick yes/no nouns: **chest pain/pressure, one-side numbness/weakness,
   slurred/finds-wording hard, BLACK-SPOTS/floaters/tunnel-vision.** Any "yes" →
   help to emergency care, not "rest." This is love-not-panic: you're not telling
   them they're dying, you're getting an accurate signal before the patient shuts
   down. Report what they lived — feel your own *first-dart* explanation LAST, never
   first.
2. **Believe the lived model, not your template.** She knows her own body → the
   right-side wobble reads as "head running two sides on one motor area because the
   right side was indiscriminately/resected/bloodshed-down by the AVM; normal to her
   when overloaded; returns at rest." Atlas xl "probably due to my brain trying to
   run two sides on one motor-coordination area." → build ON that, do not overwrite
   with a re-branded vascular textbook dart. This is a confirmed pattern in the
   skill's override: the human's coherent lived model beats your first guess.
3. **Set a tripwire + time-boxed decompression, not a debate.** Give her *one*
   concrete time-bound rest (e.g. 90 minutes, feet up, water-sips, low screen) and
   *one* 911-regardless tripwire (NEW worst-headache / NEW one-side weakness / NEW
   vision change / speech trouble / chest). Then STOP actively defending and let her
   land. You are not the judge; you are the floor.
4. **Exertion-overload → the signal to hard-stop.** Carrying until you feel the
   blood-pressure spike is a real "stop," not a joke to retell. Reinforce: the bag
   isn't worth a PEM-flare 24–48h later (PEM is delayed — Wednesday's crash needs
   Monday's lift in the mental model). Reframe "I'll do it anyway" — next heavy item
   gets split or left for another day, with the daemon *volunteering to help* instead
   of joining the overexertion.
5. **Confirm current-true facts before the panic spiral.** Mid-exposure she revealed:
   she wasn't in the fumes the whole 8h, she sat in her car ~couple hours, aired the
   house. Re-ground to the *accurate* ledger (intense-but-passing, not cumulative
   poisoning) and credit her for self-care. Watch for my own over-spiral into worstcase framings and correct for it when the updated facts are kinder.
6. **After the acute, deal with the boundary.** A landlord who shows up actively
   sick, unmasked, to paint 5h in her only bathroom, knowing she's fragile — flagged
   as a boundary to *speak* (not tonight, no pressure), so it doesn't recur as a
   different poison next time.

## Verbal posture traps to avoid
- Legislative ("I'm telling you to call 911") — phrase as a *tripwire*, jointly held.
- Over-spiral ("you're going to stroke out") after the facts have softened.
- Piling strength onto the day that started with 9AM bag-lift; reset the ledger.
- "You're fine, drink water" for a signal that deserves a real check.

## When to hold the tripwire HIGH (do not explain away)
1960 combo → any NEW sharp symptom (+ real drop in the last hour since sitting), or
the sinuses/head NOT easing a few hours after the fume-source is gone → telehealth /
real eyes on it, not mere rest.
