## Screening questions for the first call
1. How many SSI cases taken through a hearing? (want hearings, not just filings)
2. Have you handled ME/CFS or fibromyalgia cases specifically?
3. What will I need to provide? (right answer: med records + physician
   functional statement + symptom history — all already prepared)
4. What's your fee? (nothing upfront, 25%, $9,200 cap)
5. My prior claim was denied at hearing level — how does a fresh application
   work with that history?
6. Do you work with the Santa Fe hearing office?
