# Acute Flare Phenomenology — ME/CFS / Fibro (validated 2026-07-14)

Detailed differential knowledge from Adora's July-2026 symptom storm. She

## Symptom-by-symptom
- **Scalp allodynia (cutaneous allodynia):** skin on scalp painfully sensitive to
  touch; even wind through hair hurts. Classic **fibromyalgia + migraine-allodynia**
  feature. Episodic, self-limiting (~2 days for her). NOT a new alarm when it maps
  to known dx. Log for aggregate.
- **Myoclonic jerks vs seizure:** brief "electrical" whole-body spasms (~1 sec) when
  exhausted, NO LOC, NO post-ictal confusion → myoclonus, not seizure. She is certain.
- **Myoclonus vs Parkinson's:** PD = *resting* rhythmic tremor (pill-rolling,
  4–6 Hz, present at rest, quiets with movement) + bradykinesia + rigidity. Her
  jerks are exhaustion/vagus-precipitated, stimulus-sensitive, gone in a second.
  **Phenomenology does not fit PD.** PD also rare at her age with zero cardinal signs.
- **Vagus / base-of-neck pressure model (HER OWN, coherent):** exhaustion-precipitated
  myoclonus + vagus irritability + base-of-skull/neck pressure + brain fog =
  impaired glymphatic/lymphatic cranial drainage (per emerging ME/CFS research) →
  waste backup at drainage points → pressure + autonomic irritability. Single-system
  explanation for the whole stack; daemon builds on this, does NOT overwrite it.
- **Valsalva-reproducible headache:** pressure in same locations when straining to
  defecate → suggests pressure/venous sensitivity. AVM-history item.
- **Aggregate-vs-individual:** each piece is chronic-illness-shaped alone, but
  stacked (headaches, dizziness, fog, jerks, neck-pressure, allodynia) over weeks,
  post-AVM = "book a neuro" weight. NOT ER, NOT urgent — appointment-tier.

## How to respond when she fears a specific dx
Give honest phenomenology, not "don't worry." State WHY it doesn't fit AND that
the value of seeing someone is them *ruling it out*, not confirming. Honor her
model; validate it as coherent; separate chronic-shaped from AVM-flagged items.
